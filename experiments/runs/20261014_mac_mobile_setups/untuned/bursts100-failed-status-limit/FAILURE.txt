Failed run: untuned model, bursts of 100 (2026-10-14, 19:17-19:50 IST). Kept as evidence; not used for results.

What happened
- Burst L100-combined: 100 requests were sent at once and all 100 were committed on the ledger.
- The load script asked the backend for the preparation status of all 100 requests in one call.
  The status route accepts at most 50 request ids per call and answered HTTP 400
  ("ids must be 1 to 50 request ids separated by commas"), three times in a row.
- The script therefore marked all 100 rows as failed at the stage "recommendation" and the
  auditor decided none of them during the burst (rows.jsonl, error field).
- After the burst the script closed the leftovers by following their advice on the Mac
  (bursts.json, closedAfterBurst): 98 were closed, 2 timed out after 5 minutes because the
  model was still working through the queue of 100.
- The next burst did not start: the host was not idle (2 waiting), so the script stopped
  ("the host is not idle: 2 waiting, 0 authorizations active") and run-campaign-100.sh
  stopped before switching to V7 (campaign.log, 19:50:25).
- The 2 waiting requests were closed by hand at 19:51 IST by following their advice
  (closed-by-hand.json): REQ-059db6f9a3ba3347 FORCE_ALLOW (advice ALLOW), REQ-0e917667cdfc3a65
  FORCE_DENY (advice DENY).

Cause and fix
- The bursts of 5, 10 and 25 never needed more than 50 ids, so the trial runs did not show it.
  The one-hour runner already asked in groups of 50.
- Fixed in experiments/e2e/setup-load.js (statusInChunks: groups of 50, with a test), and the
  wait for leftovers was raised to 45 minutes. The bursts of 100 were then run again from the start.
